## Üretim istemi

Use case: compositing / background-extraction. Create one polished photographic product cutout asset for the hero of a Dosso Dossi Coffee Flutter app. Image 1 is our existing orange-lid cream takeaway coffee cup reference. Image 2 is the exact official brand logo to place on its front. Preserve the branding faithfully, with the male figure wearing hat and sunglasses and exact text 'DOSSO DOSSI' above, 'COFFEE' below, not a newly invented logo. A single upright full cup with vibrant #EF6024 orange lid, ivory paper body, natural soft studio light, logo facing camera straight-on. A few realistic roasted coffee beans around the base and two restrained green coffee leaf sprigs behind the cup, resembling the hero composition in a premium cream coffee-wallet app. Cup dominates image; full lid and base visible; no clipping. Actual transparent alpha background, no white rectangle, no checkerboard. Compact portrait composition tightly bounded by the cup and leaves, soft minimal contact shadow only, camera at cup mid height, no extra text, no UI, no phone frame, no watermark. Production-ready PNG transparent cutout for a 200px wide mobile illustration.
